FLAUNT Takedown and Appeals Process

Effective 21 October 2026 · Version 1.0

This page explains how to ask Flaunt Technologies L.L.C-FZ ("FLAUNT") to remove content that infringes your rights, and how to appeal a moderation decision. All requests go to biz@flaunt.ae. Our designated agent for copyright notices is the operator at the address in the Contact section.

1. Reporting content that breaks the Community Guidelines

Use the Report button on the post, comment or profile in the app. It is the fastest route: reports go straight to our review queue and are acted on within 24 hours. You do not need to email us.

2. Copyright complaints

If you believe content on FLAUNT infringes your copyright, email biz@flaunt.ae with the subject "Copyright complaint" and include:

  1. A link to, or the username and description of, the content.
  2. A description of the work you say is infringed.
  3. Your name, postal address and email.
  4. A statement that you believe in good faith the use is not authorised by the copyright owner, their agent or the law.
  5. A statement, made under penalty of perjury where that applies, that the information is accurate and that you are the owner or authorised to act for the owner.
  6. Your signature (typed is acceptable).

We acknowledge complete complaints within 2 business days, remove or disable access to the content promptly on receipt of a valid complaint, and notify the user who posted it. That user may send a counter-notice with the same categories of information and a statement, under penalty of perjury where that applies, that the content was removed by mistake. If we receive a valid counter-notice and you do not tell us within 10 business days that you have started legal action, we may restore the content.

Accounts that repeatedly infringe copyright are closed.

3. Likeness and privacy complaints

If a clip shows you and you did not agree to be filmed or posted, email biz@flaunt.ae with the subject "Likeness complaint", a link to or description of the content, how we can identify you in it, and whether you want it removed or just blurred. We acknowledge within 2 business days and act within 5. Complaints involving anyone under 16 are handled immediately.

4. Appealing a moderation decision

If content of yours was hidden, removed or refused, or your account was restricted, suspended or closed, and you believe the decision was wrong, email biz@flaunt.ae with the subject "Appeal", your username, and what was removed or restricted. Say why you think the decision was wrong.

We reply within 5 business days with one of three outcomes: the decision is reversed and the content or access restored; the decision stands, with the reason; or we need more information. Every appeal is reviewed by a person. Content removed by moderation is kept for 30 days so that a successful appeal can restore it; after 30 days it is permanently deleted and cannot be restored.

Decisions under the "involves someone under 16" rule are not restored on appeal unless age is proven.

5. Legal requests

Requests from law enforcement or courts should be sent to biz@flaunt.ae with the subject "Legal request". We respond to valid requests as the law requires and preserve content where we are obliged to.
