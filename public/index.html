Flaunt Technologies is a Dubai-based software company. We design, build and run social media applications, starting with FLAUNT, a camera app for the gym.

What we do

Three licensed activities, one product.

Build

Social media application development and management. We ship native iPhone software and the services behind it.

Grow

Marketing services via social media. Our apps are made for sharing, so distribution is part of the product.

Hold

Flaunt Technologies is the holding company for the FLAUNT brand, its software and its intellectual property.

Our product

FLAUNT

F

Film your lift. Keep your music. Share in under two minutes.

FLAUNT is an iPhone app for lifters. It records your set while your own music keeps playing, lands an AI soundtrack on the moment that matters, and sends the clip to Instagram, TikTok or anywhere else.
